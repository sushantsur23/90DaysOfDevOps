# 90-Day DevOps & Cloud Learning Plan

## Current Level

I am a working DevOps professional with 2–3 years of experience. I have practical exposure to DevOps concepts, CI/CD, cloud environments, automation, and deployment workflows. My next step is to strengthen my fundamentals, improve my troubleshooting ability, and become more confident in designing and implementing production-ready DevOps solutions.

## My 90-Day Goals

### 1. Build and Deploy a Production-Ready Application

Deploy a containerized application on AWS using Docker and Kubernetes, with proper configuration, networking, monitoring, and basic security practices. The goal is to understand the complete application lifecycle rather than simply getting an application running.

### 2. Build a Reliable CI/CD Pipeline

Create an end-to-end **CI/CD pipeline** that automatically builds, tests, scans, and deploys an application. I want to gain stronger practical understanding of pipeline design, artifact management, environment configuration, secrets handling, and deployment strategies.

### 3. Improve Cloud & Infrastructure Skills

Build and manage AWS infrastructure using **Terraform**, while improving my understanding of IAM, VPC, compute, storage, networking, monitoring, and cost-conscious infrastructure design.

## Three Core DevOps Skills I Want to Build

- Linux & Troubleshooting: Become more confident in diagnosing system, networking, process, storage, permissions, and application-related issues.

- CI/CD & Automation: Design maintainable pipelines and automate repetitive development, testing, security, and deployment activities.

- Kubernetes & Containerization: Strengthen my understanding of Docker, Kubernetes architecture, deployments, services, networking, resource management, probes, and troubleshooting.

## Weekly Time Budget

I will dedicate approximately 15–18 hours per week to learning and hands-on practice:

- Weekdays: 2–2.5 hours per day
- Weekends: 4–6 hours per day

My priority will be hands-on implementation rather than passive learning. I will use labs, personal projects, troubleshooting exercises, documentation, and small experiments to reinforce each concept.

## Consistency Commitment

I will follow a simple cycle:

Learn → Practice → Troubleshoot → Document → Repeat

I do not expect to master everything in 90 days. The objective is to make measurable progress every week and build the habit of continuous learning. Even on busy days, I will try to spend some time reviewing, practicing, or documenting what I have learned.
